XRISM spectroscopy

Resolving narrow and broadened emission-line structures around obscured AGNs using the Resolve microcalorimeter.

AGN circumnuclear structure

Connecting Fe Kα line profiles with the torus, inner torus, BLR, and circumnuclear gas geometry.

Multiwavelength modeling

Combining X-ray spectroscopy with infrared and other wavelength constraints to build a physical picture of gas and dust around supermassive black holes.
