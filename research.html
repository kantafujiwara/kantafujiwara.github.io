Research

My work focuses on the physical structure and dynamics of gas surrounding supermassive black holes, especially in obscured active galactic nuclei.

High-resolution Fe K spectroscopy

I use XRISM/Resolve to separate velocity components of fluorescent Fe K emission and investigate where the line is produced within the nuclear region.

核近傍の構造

By modeling line widths, reflection spectra, absorption, and geometry, I investigate the relationship between the dusty torus, its inner edge, and the broad-line region.

Ionized gas and outflows

Photoionization modeling with tools such as SPEX/PION is used to constrain ionization state, column density, velocity, and velocity dispersion of circumnuclear gas.

X-ray / infrared connection

I am interested in linking X-ray reflection and absorption with infrared emission from the torus and polar dusty structures to obtain a unified view of AGN environments.
